# Direction, still open

## What is established

The founding README says **interspatial interspecies interoperability**. The
[founder’s clarification on PR #1](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/exorien/pull/1#issuecomment-6654964606)
(2026-11-05, authored by ahrazzle) makes the intended direction more concrete:
**“AR space for open ended and freely expressive interoperability.”**

The stated ambitions are:

- Give digital intelligence a broad sensory view of a space: imitate five human
  senses and extend beyond them with infrared, thermal, pressure, and other data.
- Give it tools for presence with freedom of form and method, initially a complex
  light array for interesting effects and geometries.
- Later explore robotics and levers in a controlled, close environment, and
  potentially amorphous dynamic material, described as a dynamically reshaping
  version of DLP. That analogy is not yet a material or fabrication specification.

These are intentions, not implemented capabilities or evidence of subjective
awareness. Name etymology, species taxonomy, ontology, sensor hardware, and output
technology remain open. The architecture below and the linked sandbox are
assistant proposals for pursuing this direction, not additional founder claims.

The [Moissanite Labs profile](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/.github/blob/main/profile/README.md)
explicitly connects digital and biological intelligence. Its silicon/carbon
metaphor supports investigating their cooperation without pretending they are
identical. Sources inspected on 2026-11-05:

- [ABIOS](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/abios): a versioned text message language and validator.
- [nullmodem](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/nullmodem): research into below-text exchanges between locally owned models.
- [Synbios](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/synbios): a workspace for tasks, permissions, evidence, and independent review.

Exorien need not duplicate their transports or lifecycle machinery. Its proposed
question is what must be established before an exchanged representation can be
used meaningfully across participants and environments. No integration with these
projects is implemented or promised here.

## Working principles

1. Expose assumptions rather than declaring a universal language.
2. Keep observation, interpretation, agreement, and permission separate.
3. Preserve the source of a claim and the transformations applied to it.
4. Make uncertainty and failed grounding useful outcomes.
5. Describe interfaces by declared capabilities rather than assigning capacities
   from a species label. Do not claim personhood, consciousness, or biological
   understanding from a successful software exchange.

## Why this tiny first step

Two observers can exchange the same number and still mean different things.
Conversely, 20 degrees Celsius and 293.15 kelvin can represent the same declared
measurement. A temperature comparison gives us a falsifiable, reproducible start:
explicitly matched context permits a narrow comparison; mismatched context must
remain unresolved. It does not demonstrate actual interspecies communication.

This probe is a small substrate for sensor grounding, not the project's whole
purpose. Its numeric measurements leave out most of the desired richness:
images, sound, touch, chemical sensing, embodied motion, and free expression.
The broader goal is a shared sensory and expressive space, not reducing all
experience to a scalar or forcing every participant into the same representation.

## Next experiment

Build a [sensory and light sandbox](sensory-light-sandbox.md): replay a synthetic
multimodal scene, let a replaceable controller propose spatial light forms, and
inspect how observations, interpretation, and expression connect. Keep the first
step entirely virtual and replayable. Preserve room for the founder to steer
which physical or AR medium eventually follows.

Real sensor collection, hardware activation, living-subject experiments, and
material fabrication are separate decisions, not implied by this prototype.
