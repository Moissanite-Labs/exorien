# Direction, still open

## What is established

The founding README says **interspatial interspecies interoperability**. No name
etymology, species taxonomy, ontology, or implementation was specified. Everything
below is a working research proposal, not a claim about the founder's beliefs.

The [Moissanite Labs profile](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/.github/blob/main/profile/README.md)
explicitly connects digital and biological intelligence. Its silicon/carbon
metaphor supports investigating their cooperation without pretending they are
identical. Sources inspected on 2026-11-05:

- [ABIOS](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/abios): a versioned text message language and validator.
- [nullmodem](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/nullmodem): research into below-text exchanges between locally owned models.
- [Synbios](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/synbios): a workspace for tasks, permissions, evidence, and independent review.

Exorien need not duplicate their transports or lifecycle machinery. Its proposed
question is what must be established before an exchanged representation can be
used meaningfully across participants and environments. No integration with these
projects is implemented or promised here.

## Working principles

1. Expose assumptions rather than declaring a universal language.
2. Keep observation, interpretation, agreement, and permission separate.
3. Preserve the source of a claim and the transformations applied to it.
4. Make uncertainty and failed grounding useful outcomes.
5. Describe interfaces by declared capabilities rather than assigning capacities
   from a species label. Do not claim personhood, consciousness, or biological
   understanding from a successful software exchange.

## Why this tiny first step

Two observers can exchange the same number and still mean different things.
Conversely, 20 degrees Celsius and 293.15 kelvin can represent the same declared
measurement. A temperature comparison gives us a falsifiable, reproducible start:
explicitly matched context permits a narrow comparison; mismatched context must
remain unresolved. It does not demonstrate actual interspecies communication.

The proposed longer path stays open: reference frames, time, sensory channels,
uncertain interpretations, correction, and participant-controlled boundaries.
Physical, virtual, and perceptual spaces are candidate meanings of interspatial,
not definitions settled by this prototype. Nonhuman biological interfaces would
need domain expertise, ethical review, and evidence before experiments involving
living subjects. None are included.

## Next decision point

Choose a real, bounded encounter and ask what failure the current experiment
cannot represent. Candidate: a human and a simulated robot referring to the same
object from different coordinate frames. First define the evidence needed to
establish that shared referent; do not silently turn a coordinate transform into
proof of shared understanding. Develop one measured example before a protocol,
platform, or broad ontology.
